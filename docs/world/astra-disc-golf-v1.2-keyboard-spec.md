# Island Disc Golf v1.2: keyboard-first traversal

**Status:** implemented on \`feat/sids-world\`; needs full CI and physical desktop/mobile playtest. This supersedes \`astra-disc-golf-v1-spec.md\` for user interaction and the course route. No changes to the main world movement, games, biome contract, cave, waterfall, photo archive, or swimming.

## Product intent

The disc golf course should make the player visit different parts of the island and read the terrain, not manipulate a dashboard. The first playtest made precision input the challenge rather than course navigation. Its moving targets, angle slider, aim slider, oscillating power lock, and second accuracy lock created five interacting sources of error. v1.2 removes the moving-target and accuracy-timing demands; wind and trees remain visible, learnable environmental hazards.

## Interaction contract

While disc golf is active, WASD does **not** move the avatar. The player stands at the valid lie, or watches a disc in flight:

| Control | Response |
| --- | --- |
| A/D | Aim left/right in 2° increments relative to the fixed chains |
| W/S | Raise/lower the loft in 2° increments, clamped to 6°..42° |
| Tap Space | Throw at the highlighted suggested power, with centered release |
| Hold Space | Charge a single meter; release to throw with manually chosen power |
| On-screen button | The same tap/hold behavior for pointer/touch and keyboard Enter |
| Numeric hole buttons | Optional course selection, disabled during flight |
| Exit | Restore normal exploration and remove course-only visualization |

A faint flight preview is generated by the same deterministic simulation used by the live throw. The recommendation searches trial powers against real terrain, coastline and wind at the current lie; it is a suggestion, not guaranteed chain capture. It does not claim to predict impact with all placed tree trunks. Feedback is primarily spatial: predicted flight arc, landing ring, distance to fixed chains, light at the actual lie, and visible throw animation. No second release accuracy meter, extra camera mode, or random miss.

## Flight and fair recovery

- The low end of the speed curve now supports short putts; the previous minimum launch velocity was suitable for a drive, not a two-meter putt.
- A swept proximity check catches discs in the chain volume even between simulation substeps.
- A landed shot outside the catch zone always creates a playable lie; near the basket it prompts an easy Space putt.
- Water and out-of-bounds return to the previous lie without counting a stroke. Tree collision counts a stroke and puts the next lie outside the walker collision radius.
- A recommendation comes from repeated deterministic previews. Accuracy remains precisely centered for tap and hold releases; the player adjusts power and direction intentionally.

## Regression and human signoff

Automated checks: four stationary targets, each distance >45 world units, linked tees, stable wind, power and loft flight sensitivity, swept chain capture, short-putt recommendation, preview/live parity, water refunds, trunk contacts, graphics cleanup. CI must pass typecheck, lint, tests, and production build.

Human checks: first tee and next three transitions; WASD/Space without pointer use; Space held/released under keyboard repeat; click/touch press behavior; at least one realistic putt under 3m; tree/water recoveries; mobile viewport without covering player; target visibility from a distant tee; frame time/resource cleanup; no leakage into ordinary WASD/Space after exit. A green test suite alone does not establish visual or interaction acceptance.

## Basket-first camera introduction (2026-10-10)

Each time a visitor starts a hole, the camera cuts to a close view of the **stationary basket** from the fairway side. Hold the chains clearly on screen for ~0.7 seconds, then move over the hole's real terrain for ~2.65 seconds, settling in the exact ordinary third-person aiming view behind the player. This is a **camera-only** introduction: shots, aim, power, strokes, wind and player position do not change.

- Use deterministic world simulation time, which pauses with menus/hidden tabs, not a free-running timeout.
- Ease smoothly between basket and player, rise above the fairway and sample terrain beneath the moving camera; avoid clipping through hills. The last frame must match the existing normal third-person position and look direction, without a jarring second jump.
- The camera starts **on the basket**, rather than easing from the previous location to the basket. There is no extra loading pause.
- WASD, arrows, Space or Escape cancels the introduction and immediately restores the normal camera. A shot also cancels it, so gameplay always wins.
- Honor reduced-motion: show the basket briefly, then **cut** to the player. No continuous flyover.
- Replay only when selecting/starting a new hole, not when landing, retrying water, putting, changing loft, or reopening the HUD. Exiting, fast travel and menu pause clear the introduction.
- QA: screenshot first basket frame, mid-fairway reveal and settled tee camera on all four holes; verify occlusion by trees, rocks and mountains; check reduced motion, immediate throws, pause/resume, hole switches and clean World exit.
- The canvas exposes `data-disc-golf-intro` (`basket`, `pullback`, `complete`, `off`) for playtest automation. User-visible UI remains restrained; there is no mandatory confirmation or tutorial overlay.

## Disc Golf v1.3: nine holes, comfortable putting, elastic release (2026-10-10)

### Design priorities
1. **Putting remains an over-the-shoulder game, not a basket close-up.** When chains are 10 world units or closer, the player's aiming camera stays ~21 units horizontally behind and ~15 units above the standing lie, biased over the shoulder. The look-ahead is limited to the playable green. During short putt flights the camera remains anchored instead of chasing the disc. Long drives may still track the disc.
2. **No surprise zoom caused by trees.** The old horizontal tree proximity check shortened camera distance down to 25% and effectively hid the player. Disc-golf cameras preserve their boom and are lifted above terrain. World navigation camera obstacle avoidance is unchanged outside disc golf. The camera intro's last shot now resolves to this same comfortable pose.
3. **Nine fixed holes form a loop.** Preserve the initial four; add five linked segments after Coastal Return, ending at the first tee. Each spot must be on dry finite terrain. A full nine-hole game should span different island biomes and both long- and short-form approaches, not nine identical drives.
4. **The Space button is the only throw interaction.** A tap is a clean suggested shot; holding grows the character's backswing and drives one elastic power meter that gently cycles around the suggested power. Release at the light band for a clean controlled throw, or intentionally earlier/later for a soft/strong shot. There is no separate accuracy meter, forced quick-time sequence, or hidden RNG.

## Disc Golf v1.4: nine-hole round authority and evidence-driven course review

### Visitor experience

- A **nine-hole scorecard** records actual chain catches only; selecting or restarting a hole does not invent a result.
- The compact HUD shows completed-hole count, aggregate strokes and score relative to the par for **played holes**. Open **Scorecard** to see individual par, recorded strokes and +/-.
- After a basket catch, the next button selects the next uncompleted hole, with wraparound only across still-unplayed holes. On completing all nine, show the finished-round total and a clear **Play another round** action, not an automatic replay.
- Replaying a completed basket is allowed for practice; its original recorded score remains unchanged. Starting a fresh round clears individual scores, retains the best complete-round total and requires confirmation when abandoning partial progress.
- The current nine-hole round survives leaving World and a page refresh via versioned local storage, with strict validation of saved values. This is local-only and makes no claim of a cloud leaderboard.

### Fairway evidence and acceptance

`lib/world/discGolfFairway.ts` samples each straight tee-to-basket segment against the island's analytic heightfield. It reports distance, elevation change, representative biomes, maximum sampled slope, basket slope, water crossings and proximity to discovery landmarks. `scripts/audit-disc-golf.ts` publishes `artifacts/world/disc-golf-fairways.json` and a human-readable markdown report in World CI.

The audit is **not visual validation**. Procedural tree trunk collisions, fallen logs, boulder instances, camera occlusion, scenic markers, true foot-path reachability, and bunker-like lie recovery still need real browser inspection. Surface risks are review flags, not proof that the disc trajectory is physically obstructed.

### QA required before merge

1. Start and complete hole 1, switch to a different tee, replay completed hole 1, and confirm it cannot corrupt the original result.
2. Complete all nine in nonsequential order; verify the next-unplayed selector, final score, par and best-round update.
3. Exit/reload/resume a partially played round and confirm all previously completed scores survive; start new round and confirm the last best remains.
4. Check small-screen scorecard scrolling and focus, clean collapse/expand, large enough nine-hole buttons, and no world key leakage.
5. Visually inspect all tees, baskets and putting lies in desktop/mobile browser; relocate any basket obstructed by a tree, steep face, discoverable object, cave shell, or activity trail. Capture screenshots and confirm the actual intended par per hole.


## Disc Golf v1.5: clear opener, playful names, Shasta fetch

- Hole 1 starts at (38,9), ends at (43,43), and the final hole returns to this opening tee. This deliberately leaves the former rock- and trunk-obstructed redwood tee. A narrow analytic capsule along the opening fairway is excluded from procedural boulders, trunks, rainforest canopy and understory; do **not** clear trees throughout the world. Verify the actual rendered line and shot trajectories in a browser before releasing.
- Player-facing title is simply **Disc Golf**. Keep the HUD restrained: hole/par, remaining distance, wind, WASD angle/loft and Space pullback. Avoid explanatory paragraphs or territorial branding.
- Each round gets a cosmetic seed for **nine distinct funny hole names**. Names are stable across replays, saved scores and refreshes, and reroll on starting a fresh round. Original hole identifiers and coordinates stay stable for score/science contracts. No unseeded name changes during a throw.
- Each actual disc release initiates Shasta's fetch. While flying, he heads toward the live disc. On ground contact he races to the real lie (or the basket if holed), sniffs briefly and wags before returning to player-follow behavior. Reuse existing obstacle-aware, terrain-following, finite-acceleration motion. Do not teleport Shasta or alter the disc, lie, strokes, or camera when playing normally. Water/out-of-bounds, new hole, exit and prolonged inability to reach the disc safely cancel the fetch.
- The camera gently pans along with Shasta's chase, staying high enough above the terrain and blending back to ordinary framing after he finds the disc. On short putts keep the established player-focused elevated third-person view with only a small look adjustment. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by disabling the additional fetch-camera pan. Never block WASD/Space while Shasta is finding the disc.
- Expose `data-dog-fetch-stage` and `data-disc-golf-fetch-camera` on canvas for manual/browser regression. Inspect throws, nearby putts, very long shots, trunk collisions, water retries, pause/exit, fast travel and course switches. Confirm cleanup and the absence of dog chase behavior outside Disc Golf.
