Ecological Synergy

The first ten rooms still teach a bounded ecology one idea at a time. v0.10 adds composition rather than more buttons: reflected fire, fungi, gas, terrain, temporary forage, enemy behavior, and boss routing can now interact while the eight-key Countercut grammar stays fixed.

ecological countercut grammar

Movement commits. Countercut redirects danger. Synergy lets one good read reshape the room.

01 · step-dash

Tap WASD for a cardinal burst. A second tap made during the current burst stays queued after key release and executes after the first commitment.

02 · cut

Arrow keys swing the machete in four directions. Cuts travel with Sprid during a dash, so movement and attack vectors remain independent.

03 · counter

Cut toward the side a projectile actually reaches you from. Hostile motion normalizes into an 840 px/s return, or 1040 px/s on a perfect Countercut.

04 · chain a return

A reflected projectile can trigger a mushroom without spending the return. A wave-origin Countercut blooms toxic spores farther, making projectile history tactically meaningful after reflection.

05 · shear spores

Cut a gas cloud during a committed dash or its short echo window to shove the cloud in the cut direction. Verdant Flow makes the ecological shove stronger, not the base dash or Countercut.

06 · read the hunters

Surveyors and other cautious units reject gas-filled escape space and steer away from dangerous footing. Skidders remain reckless, so baiting them through poison and shards stays viable.

07 · forage

Clear grass, deadwood, or rubble for Heartleaf, Rush Resin, Barkguard, Edge Stone, or Flow Sap. Temporary finds reward curiosity without permanent power inflation.

08 · verdant flow

Perfect Countercuts, Crosscuts, Long Returns, terrain routes, hazard finishes, and spore routes build a short synergy chain. At high Flow, three linked reads ignite Verdant Flow.

09 · read the boss

PAC-a-Saw vents heat before major attacks and now bulldozes deadwood and rubble it physically crosses. The machine can accidentally manufacture your next route.

10 · visual priority

Only the most immediate hostile projectile receives the threat halo. Friendly returns and Sprid remain visually dominant over gas, particles, and decorative terrain as rooms become denser.
