Environmental Resonance

Sprid is a machete-wielding forest runner fighting clear-cut operations across living arenas. Protect trees, Countercut hostile patterns, reshape routes, and explore while the fight is still alive. Tall grass, deadwood, rubble, fungi, ice, mud, water, sand, and brambles can hide advantages or become weapons. The ecology obeys the same rules for Sprid and his enemies.

The first ten rooms teach terrain and forest chemistry one concept at a time. Exploration is optional but tactically rewarding. Deeper rooms deterministically remix enemies, projectile geometry, route pressure, fungi, forage, and terrain without changing the eight-key Countercut grammar.

environmental countercut grammar

Movement commits. Cutting routes danger. Curiosity changes the fight.

01 · step-dash

Tap WASD for a cardinal burst. A second tap made during the current burst stays queued after key release and executes after the first commitment.

02 · cut

Arrow keys swing the machete in four directions. Cuts travel with Sprid during a dash, so movement and attack vectors remain independent.

03 · counter

Cut toward the side a projectile actually reaches you from. Its hostile curve disappears and it launches back at 840 px/s, or 1040 px/s on a perfect Countercut.

04 · route fire

Returned bullets can hit any enemy. Crosscuts, Long Returns, penetration, and environmental knockback reward deliberate formation reads.

05 · read terrain

Ice extends commitment. Mud and sand add drag. Water slows. Brambles and shattered ice stagger. Ground rules apply to enemies too.

06 · forage

Fully clear grass, chop deadwood, or break rubble to discover short-lived advantages. Heartleaf heals, resin accelerates cadence, Barkguard blocks a hit, Edge Stone extends reach, and Flow Sap restores Flow.

07 · read fungi

Friendly caps have clean luminous colors. Yellow-green Venomcaps and uncanny Ghostcaps vent spores when cut. Toxic clouds hurt Sprid and enemies alike, so timing matters.

08 · fracture

Cut during a dash, or immediately after one, to fracture ice crossed by that committed movement. The shards become a symmetric hazard instead of a free projectile erase.

09 · hunt evasive units

Foremen and Chairs backstep; Lobbyists, Brokers, and Surveyors blink. Mud, brambles, shards, and carefully triggered gas can turn escape space into a trap.

10 · survive the run

Heartwood persists. Perfect Groves heal one heartwood. Flow and temporary field finds reward players who keep moving, reading, and reshaping each arena.
