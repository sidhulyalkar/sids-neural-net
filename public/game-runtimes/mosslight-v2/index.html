    The first 30 arenas are fixed and seeded. Learn the layouts, find useful side routes, and push deeper. After arena 30, the run continues into deterministic endless rooms.

    how to play

    Simple inputs. Difficult rooms.

    Move

    WASD commits to a short cardinal dash. One extra direction can stay queued while the current dash finishes.

    Cut / reflect

    Arrow keys cut in four directions. Cut toward the side an incoming shot reaches you from to send it back. Precise timing makes the return faster.

    Use the room

    Mud, water, ice, bramble, rubble, grass, poison, and breakable routes affect both sides. Later arenas are built around combining these rules.

    Explore

    Deadwood, grass, rubble, and mushrooms can reveal healing, speed, shield, reach, flow, or poison. These are temporary choices, not permanent upgrades.
