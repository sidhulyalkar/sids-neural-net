    how to play

    Glide. Charge. Sweep. Return fire.

    Glide

    Hold any WASD combination for continuous acceleration and smooth diagonal steering. Release to coast briefly; slick surfaces preserve more momentum.

    Charge dash

    Hold Space to store burst energy while you keep steering. Release to launch along the current input vector, or along your existing momentum when no direction is held. Space can also queue just before dash recovery ends.

    Tongue arc

    Arrow keys choose the direction of a wide tongue sweep. Every swing has a short wind-up, an active moving arc, and recovery. A committed dash can cancel directly into the blade after its opening burst.

    Parry

    The first five active simulation ticks, about 42 ms, are the parry window. Meet incoming fire there to launch a 1160 px/s return that hits harder and can pierce. The later sweep still cuts enemies and the pond, but it does not reflect projectiles.

    Read enemies

    Skimmers orbit, Striders evade, Dragonflies fire high-speed lanes, and Shellbacks armor their front. Flank, redirect fire, or reposition instead of mashing attacks.

    Use the pond

    Water currents, mud, reeds, poison, algae, stones, and reflected shots reshape each fight. Strong runs use the environment to split overlapping threats.
