    how to play

    Move with intent. Create the opening.

    Carve space

    Hold any WASD combination for continuous acceleration. Momentum matters, but your movement remains fully controllable between committed actions.

    Charge dash

    Hold Space while steering, then release into an exponential burst. A committed dash can bend modestly but cannot pivot freely, so choose a line with an exit before launching.

    Reactive Blade

    Arrow keys choose the direction of a 156° tongue sweep. The tongue is physically anchored to Sprid’s mouth while its combat geometry remains centered on the authoritative player root.

    Perfect parry

    The first five active simulation ticks, about 42 ms, reflect incoming fire at 1160 px/s. Perfect returns hit harder, pierce once, add Flow, and refund 100 ms of dash cooldown.

    Bait and punish

    Skimmers, Striders, and Dragonflies react on fixed timings. Force the evade, read the shrinking opening ring, then punish before the next orchestrated threat beat arrives.

    Break bosses

    Perfect returns, hazard routes, and dash-cuts through a boss telegraph crack guard segments. A broken guard exposes the core for a short high-damage blade window while existing projectiles remain dangerous.

    Use the pond

    Currents, mud, reeds, poison, brambles, shards, deadwood, and reflected shots are combat tools. Strong runs solve positioning puzzles instead of treating the arena as decoration.

    Read the rhythm

    Heavy attacks enter a deterministic room-level queue. Learn each room’s call-and-response cadence, identify the lead threat, and choose whether to parry, reposition, bait, or route the response.
