Environmental Resonance

Sprid is a machete-wielding forest runner fighting clear-cut operations across living arenas. Protect trees, chop routes through deadwood and tall grass, read enemy commitments, and turn hostile projectile patterns into high-speed weapons. Ice, mud, sand, water, brambles, and breakable barriers obey the same rules for Sprid and his enemies, so the ground becomes another part of the Countercut formation.

environmental countercut grammar

Movement commits. Cutting routes danger. Terrain shapes both.

01 · step-dash

Tap WASD for a cardinal burst. A second tap made during the current burst remains queued after key release and executes after the first commitment.

02 · cut

Arrow keys swing the machete in four cardinal directions. Cuts travel with Sprid during a dash, so movement and attack vectors remain independent.

03 · counter

Cut toward the side a projectile actually reaches you from. Its hostile curve disappears and it launches as a much faster return.

04 · route fire

Returned bullets can hit any enemy. Crosscuts, Long Returns, penetration, and pushing enemies into hazardous terrain reward deliberate formation reads.

05 · read terrain

Ice extends commitment. Mud and sand add drag. Water slows. Brambles and shattered ice stagger. The same mobility and hazard rules apply to enemies.

06 · hunt evasive units

Foremen and Chairs backstep; Lobbyists, Brokers, and Surveyors blink. Mud, brambles, and ice shards can jam a safe escape destination.

07 · shape the forest

Chop tall grass and deadwood to clean sightlines. Break brittle root barriers with repeated cuts or a reflected projectile to manufacture new lanes.

08 · resonate

A precisely timed dash-cut can fracture ice into a dangerous shard field. Environmental transformations are contextual, so ordinary cuts do not erase projectile pressure.

09 · read patterns

Straight, zigzag, wave, spiral, swerve, and wobble bullets keep distinct silhouettes. Their counter rule remains spatial and unchanged.

10 · survive the run

Heartwood persists. Perfect Groves heal one heartwood. Flow rewards counters, grazes, and smart routing as dash commitment grows deeper in the forest.
