countercut grammar

Movement commits. Cutting routes danger.

01 · step-dash

Tap WASD for a short cardinal burst. Hold to repeat. A second tap made during the current burst stays queued even after key release and executes immediately when the first step finishes.

02 · cut

Arrow keys swing the machete in four cardinal directions. Cuts travel with Sprid during a dash, so movement and attack vectors can disagree.

03 · counter

Cut toward the side a projectile reaches you from. Countering removes its hostile curve and launches a much faster return with restrained target assist inside your chosen lane.

04 · route fire

Returned bullets can hit any enemy, not just the shooter. Cross-enemy hits score Crosscuts. Long return flights gain damage and score. Perfect counters can penetrate one target.

05 · read patterns

Hostile shots may zigzag, wave, spiral, swerve once, or wobble irregularly. The answer remains spatial: read the side the projectile actually arrives from.

06 · hunt evasive units

Foremen and Chairs backstep. Lobbyists, Brokers, and Surveyors can blink away. Their escape destination is telegraphed, and a returned shot staggers them out of evasion.

07 · shape the forest

Living trees and deadwood block step-dashes. Chop deadwood to manufacture lanes before a projectile pattern makes that route urgent.

08 · survive the run

Heartwood persists. Perfect Groves heal one heartwood. Flow rewards counters and close dash-grazes. Dash distance grows, making later movement stronger and harder to place.
