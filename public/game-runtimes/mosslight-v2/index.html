countercut grammar

Movement is a commitment. Cutting is independent.

01 · step-dash

Tap WASD for a short cardinal burst. Hold a direction to repeat bursts at a controlled cadence. There is no continuous drift.

02 · cut

Arrow keys swing the machete in four cardinal directions. A cut can damage enemies, chop deadwood, or catch a projectile.

03 · counter

Cut toward the side a projectile reaches you from. Early contact is a perfect counter and sends a stronger shot back.

04 · overlap

You can cut while step-dashing. The movement direction and attack direction do not need to match.

05 · protect

Some enemies prefer living trees over Sprid. If the grove is fully clear-cut, the run ends.

06 · grow

Dash distance increases through the run. Longer steps cross danger faster but punish careless positioning and overcommitment.
