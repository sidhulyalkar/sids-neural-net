A fast pond run about a frog, hungry insects, and one very useful tongue. Move with WASD. Slap with the arrow keys. Catch an incoming attack at the right moment to send it back.

    Thirty fixed pond arenas reward learning layouts and insect behavior. Reeds, mud, water, algae, poison, driftwood, and optional side routes can help or trap either side. After pond 30, the same verified run continues into deterministic endless rooms.

    how to play

    Move. Slap. Eat the opening.

    Move

    WASD commits the frog to a short cardinal burst. One extra direction can stay queued while the current move finishes.

    Tongue slap / reflect

    Arrow keys snap the tongue in four directions. Meet an incoming attack from the side it reaches you and the slap sends it back. Precise timing makes the return faster.

    Use the pond

    Mud, shallow water, slick algae, dense reeds, shell beds, poison, and breakable cover affect both sides. Harder ponds combine several at once.

    Explore

    Reeds, driftwood, stones, and mushrooms can open optional routes or temporary healing, speed, shield, reach, and flow effects. They never replace the core movement-and-reflect game.

    controls
