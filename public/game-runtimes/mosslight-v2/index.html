countercut grammar

Movement is a commitment. Cutting is independent.

01 · step-dash

Tap WASD for a short cardinal burst. Hold to repeat game-timed bursts. A fast second tap is buffered so chained movement stays responsive even during the current step.

02 · cut

Arrow keys swing the machete in four cardinal directions. Cuts travel with Sprid during a dash, so movement and attack vectors can disagree.

03 · counter

Cut toward the side a projectile reaches you from. Early contact creates a stronger white perfect counter. Nearby arrows teach the arrival side.

04 · route

Living trees and deadwood now block step-dashes. Chop deadwood to manufacture lanes before an enemy pattern makes that route urgent.

05 · read

Attack telegraphs lock their target before release. When a line appears, the enemy has committed. Move, counter, or exploit the recovery window.

06 · protect

Heartwood persists between rooms. Save every tree in a room to recover one heartwood. Lose the full grove and the run ends.

07 · flow

Perfect counters, kills, and close dash-grazes build Flow. Flow shortens cut recovery and held-step cadence without changing your learned dash distance.

08 · grow

Dash distance increases through the run. Longer steps cross danger faster but make fine positioning and obstacle routing more demanding.
