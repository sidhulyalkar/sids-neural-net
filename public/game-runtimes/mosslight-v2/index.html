    The prototype is deliberately focused: carve short survival lines, chain geometry, clear temporary visibility through regrowing undergrowth, and decide whether dangerous discoveries are worth leaving the safe route. Ranking remains paused while the new physics are qualified.

    how to play

    Move freely. Aim independently. Draw the next line.

    Move

    WASD controls body movement only. Keep drifting while you read the forest.

    Aim

    Arrow-key chords give crisp 8-way aim. The mouse gives free-angle aim anywhere in the clearing.

    Cutstep

    SPACE or left click fires immediately. There is no charge delay. The movement segment itself is the blade.

    Chain geometry

    Continue straight for THRUST, turn roughly ninety degrees for CROSSCUT, or reverse sharply for a projectile-clearing REVERSAL.

    Three segments

    Cutsteps refill slowly on their own. Counters, kills, and carving dense understory restore them much faster.

    Carve visibility

    Dense ferns and grass obscure threats. Cutstep severs them instantly. The forest regrows behind you.

    No blanket invulnerability

    The blade protects the line it actually occupies. Bad geometry can still get you hit.

    Follow temptation

    Useful discoveries glow through the mist. Reaching one should require leaving a safer line.
