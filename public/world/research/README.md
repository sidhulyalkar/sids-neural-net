# Research cave figures

These are lossless crops rendered from the publication PDFs already hosted in this repository. The complete figures and original labels are preserved, with a title, authors/year and figure number on the cave plaque. Clicking opens the source PDF at the relevant page.

- `cortico-striatal-beta-oscillations-2024.webp`: Figure 2, PDF page 9.
- `mapping-large-scale-networks-2021.webp`: Figure 1, PDF page 4.
- `chronic-multisite-probe-designs-2021.webp`: Figure 7, PDF page 15.

Render procedure: PyMuPDF, 2.5x page scale, clipped to the figure image bounds, lossless WebP. No scientific labels or values were altered. Original author/publisher attribution and reuse notices remain in the linked source papers.

## Museum expansion

- `default-mode-network-2021.webp`: original Figure 1 from https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/3f0c/8166125/ef2c033be2d9/tgab034f1.jpg (Fakhraei et al., Cerebral Cortex Communications, 2021). Lossless conversion; public domain in the US per the paper.
- `reward-certainty-2025.webp`: original Figure 1 from https://www.jneurosci.org/content/jneuro/45/40/e0858252025/F1.large.jpg (Koloski et al., Journal of Neuroscience, 2025). Lossless conversion with source attribution and publisher link.
- `probe-hardware-2021.webp`: complete Figure 1, PDF page 4, cropped at its image bounds at 3x from the locally hosted methods paper. Scientific labels unchanged.
- `learned-value-record.svg` and `operant-box-record.svg`: typographic publication records, not scientific figures. Figure sources returned 403/429 during preparation. Titles/links come from the canonical publications YAML; replace with verified original figures when available.
- `value-abstract-record.svg`: the 2022 abstract-only entry has no published figure.

All eight canonical publications are represented. The ninth exhibit is a second, distinct figure from the probe-design paper. Three rooms contain three exhibits each, with passage clearances retained.
