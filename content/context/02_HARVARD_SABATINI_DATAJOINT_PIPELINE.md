## One-liner

Led a major customer deployment for Harvard/Sabatini Lab involving multimodal neuroscience processing and visualization over ~12TB-scale datasets, enabling scientists to interpret neatly sorted data roughly 16 hours after collection.

## Narrative

This should be one of the most prominent case studies on the site. It shows Sid’s ability to lead real research infrastructure deployments, work with major labs, integrate multiple analysis algorithms, and manage large data flows across cloud resources.

The deployment involved multimodal data pipelines for calcium imaging, electrophysiology, fiber photometry, pose estimation, facial inference, and behavior. DataJoint provided the relational scientific workflow backbone, while cloud infrastructure enabled parallel processing and post-collection interpretation.
